# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the **Contributor Covenant, version 2.1** as its Code of
Conduct. The full text is published at:

- https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for
everyone, and to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse,
inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards & enforcement

The expected standards and the unacceptable behaviors are those enumerated in the
Contributor Covenant 2.1 (linked above). Instances of unacceptable behavior may be
reported privately to the project maintainer via a
[GitHub private security advisory](https://github.com/andreibesleaga/GABBE/security/advisories/new)
or by contacting the repository owner. Reports are reviewed promptly and fairly;
reporter privacy is respected. Enforcement follows the Covenant's Enforcement
Guidelines (Correction → Warning → Temporary Ban → Permanent Ban).
